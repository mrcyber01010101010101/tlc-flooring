Water Removal & Tear-Out

After a flood, the clock is running. We get standing water out and pull up soaked carpet and pad, LVP, laminate and wood flooring. Wet drywall and baseboards are cut out above the water line so the walls can dry, and the debris is hauled away.

Drying With Fans

Before anything new goes in, the room has to be dry. We set up fans to move air across floors and open walls and keep them running until the space is ready to rebuild. Closing up wet walls or putting new floors over a damp slab or subfloor is how problems come back.

Water-Damaged Floor Repair

Flooring is what we do every day. We repair and replace floors damaged by floods, leaks and plumbing failures:

  • Swollen, cupped or buckled laminate and wood floors
  • LVP that's lifting, separating or has water trapped underneath
  • Wet carpet and pad removal and replacement
  • Loose or cracked tile and damaged grout
  • Water-damaged subfloor and stair treads

When it's time for new floors, waterproof luxury vinyl plank is a smart choice for Florida homes that have seen water before.

Drywall, Trim & Rebuild

Once everything is dry, we hang and finish new drywall, install baseboards, door casing and interior doors, and lay the new flooring, so the room is ready to live in again.

Stopping Mold Before It Starts

Mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours after water gets in. The best defense is fast work: get the water out, remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad, and dry the space with fans. That's exactly what we do.

If mold has already spread across a large area (more than about 10 square feet), Florida law requires a state-licensed mold remediator for that part of the job. We'll tell you honestly if your home needs one and can point you to one, then handle the drywall, trim and new floors afterward.

Insurance Tips

  • Take photos and video of the water and damage before anything is torn out.
  • Call your insurance company or agent to start a claim as soon as you can.
  • Keep receipts for anything you spend on cleanup.
  • We give free written estimates you can share with your adjuster.
