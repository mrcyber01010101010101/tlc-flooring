Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

LVP is the most requested floor in Florida for good reason: it's waterproof, stands up to kids, pets and sand, and looks like real wood. We handle the whole job: removing old carpet or tile, prepping and leveling the subfloor, installing the plank, and finishing with transitions, quarter round or new baseboards.

Laminate & Hardwood

For the warm look of wood in living rooms and bedrooms, we install laminate and hardwood with tight seams, proper expansion gaps and clean cuts around doorways, cabinets and stairs.

Stairs & Steps

Stairs are where an installer shows their skill. We do stair treads, risers and curved steps in matching LVP or laminate with stair nosing, so the stairs flow with the rest of the floor.

Carpet, Carpet Tile & Commercial Flooring

We install carpet tile and LVP in offices, hallways, retail spaces and apartment units, and we work around your schedule to keep downtime low. Property managers and landlords call us for rental turnovers and upgrades.
