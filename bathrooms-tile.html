Tile Showers & Tub Surrounds

A shower is only as good as what's behind the tile. We take the old surround down to the studs, install cement backer board and waterproofing, then set the tile with straight lines and even grout joints. Corner showers, walk-ins, tub surrounds with accent stripes and pebble shower floors are all part of what we do.

Shower Niches & Accent Walls

Built-in niches give you a clean place for shampoo and soap, and an accent tile like mosaic or hexagon makes the shower the focal point of the bathroom.

Bathroom & Kitchen Tile Floors

Large-format porcelain, wood-look tile and classic ceramic, installed flat and level for bathrooms, laundry rooms, kitchens and entries. We also do kitchen backsplashes.

Bathroom Refresh Projects

Many jobs combine tile with flooring and repairs: a new tile floor, a shower or tub surround, drywall patches and new trim. One contractor for the whole bathroom keeps things simple.
